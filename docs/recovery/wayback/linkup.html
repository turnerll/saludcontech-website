​SaludConTech, Techqueria, CHCF Present

​The LinkUp | A Digital Health Innovators of Color Mixer

​CHCF Innovation Fund, Techqueria, and SaludConTech are joining forces to create a space for Digital Health Innovators and Explorers of color within the Technology-Enabled Healthcare Ecosystem.

​You are cordially invited to attend "The LinkUp | A Digital Health Innovators of Color Gathering" on August 2nd, 2023, at 5:30 PM. This mixer is being hosted by California Health Care Foundation Innovation Fund (CHCF) alongside Techqueria and SaludConTech. It is designed to bring together underrepresented minority digital health leaders to network and get to know one another.

​This is a unique opportunity for those interested in digital health to connect with like-minded individuals in a relaxed and welcoming environment. This gathering will provide an opportunity to meet and network with other innovators of color passionate about digital health, and will serve as the pre-event to the Founders and Funders of Color LA’s healthcare day on August 4th.

​ALL are welcome! Please RSVP to confirm your attendance. We look forward to seeing you there. :)

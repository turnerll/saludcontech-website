About Us

SaludConTech is an inclusive ecosystem-building community that provides an entrepreneurial infrastructure for Latino/as and other founders of color. We seek to build a diverse subset of future digital health visionaries by partnering with key stakeholders and social capital networks to facilitate connections and provide the opportunities and community needed to succeed.
